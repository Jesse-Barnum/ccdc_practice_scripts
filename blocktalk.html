🧟 MOB TRIVIA

The Creeper exists because of a coding accident — Notch was building a pig model, mixed up the height and length values, and got a tall green mistake instead. It stuck.

Endermen pick up and place blocks, hate rain and water, and were partly inspired by the Slender Man creepypasta.

Ghasts drop gunpowder and their sad-sounding cries were made by digitally altering a cat's meow.

Villagers have over a dozen professions, each tied to the work block they stand near — a lectern makes a librarian, a smoker makes a butcher.

🕰️ A SHORT HISTORY

MAY 2009

Markus "Notch" Persson releases the first public build of Minecraft, then just called "Cave Game."

NOV 2011

Minecraft officially launches version 1.0 at MineCon in Las Vegas.

2014

Microsoft acquires Mojang and the Minecraft franchise for 2.5 billion dollars.

2020

Minecraft surpasses 200 million copies sold, becoming the best-selling video game of all time.

TODAY

Minecraft still receives regular content updates and remains one of the most-played games on Earth.

💡 SURVIVAL TIPS

Sleep through the night in a bed to skip straight to morning and reset your spawn point at the same time.

Never dig straight down — you might drop into a cave, lava, or a ravine with no way back up.

Keep a water bucket on your hotbar; it can save you from fall damage and put out fires.

A Netherite tool or armor upgrade makes items immune to burning in lava or fire.
